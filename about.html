About the Kingdom of Lesotho

Lesotho is a high-altitude, landlocked country encircled by South Africa. Known for its breathtaking mountain passes, rivers, and rich Basotho culture, it is the only independent state in the world that lies entirely above 1,000 meters in elevation.

From the iconic Mokorotlo hat to traditional pony trekking across rugged trails, Lesotho offers tourists an authentic African alpine experience.
